What Are NBR Extensions?

Natural Beaded Rows (NBR) is a hand-tied weft extension method that adds natural-looking length and volume with minimal damage to your own hair. Custom wefts are hand-sewn onto a flat, beaded foundation row, so the extensions lie close to the scalp and move naturally with your hair.

As a Goldwell Master Colorist and certified NBR Extension Specialist, Minh color-matches and blends every installation so your extensions are nearly undetectable.

  • Flat, comfortable installation that lies close to the scalp
  • 100% human hair that can be colored, cut and styled
  • No heat, glue or chemicals
  • 8–10 weeks of wear between maintenance appointments
  • Holds up well in Houston's humid climate

Are NBR Extensions Right for You?

Great for

  • Fine to medium hair that needs volume, length or both
  • Natural hair at least 4–5 inches long
  • Healthy hair without excessive breakage
  • Clients ready for a move-up every 8–10 weeks

May not be ideal for

  • Hair shorter than 4 inches
  • Severely damaged or very fragile hair
  • Certain scalp conditions
  • Anyone who can't keep up with regular maintenance

How It Works

  1. 1. Consultation

    We assess your hair's texture, density and health, talk through your goals, color-match your extensions and plan how many rows you need. Your custom hair is ordered after this visit.

  2. 2. Installation

    Your hair is prepped and sectioned, the beaded rows are placed, and wefts are hand-sewn and then cut and blended into your natural hair. Plan for 2–4 hours.

  3. 3. Move-ups

    Every 8–10 weeks, the rows are moved up as your hair grows to keep them flat, comfortable and hidden. You'll leave your install with full aftercare instructions.

NBR Extension FAQs

With proper care, the extension hair itself lasts 6–12 months. The rows need a move-up appointment every 8–10 weeks as your natural hair grows.

When properly installed and maintained, NBR extensions cause minimal damage to natural hair. The beaded attachment method doesn't use heat, glue or chemicals.

Installation typically takes 2–4 hours, depending on how much hair is being added. First-time clients should plan for a longer appointment than reinstallations.

Yes. NBR extensions are 100% human hair, so they can be colored, cut and styled. We recommend professional coloring, and it's typically easier to go darker than lighter.

Your natural hair should be at least 4–5 inches long so it can fully conceal the beaded rows. NBR works especially well for fine to medium hair that needs length, volume or both.
