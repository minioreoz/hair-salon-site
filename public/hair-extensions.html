Our Extension Methods

NBR (Natural Beaded Rows)

Hand-tied wefts sewn onto a flat, beaded foundation row. No heat, glue or chemicals, very comfortable, and 6–8 weeks between move-ups. Ideal for natural-looking length and volume.

Bellami K-Tips

Individual strands attached with small keratin bonds. Because each strand is placed one at a time, K-Tips blend seamlessly and move like your own hair.

Bellami I-Tips

Individual strands attached with micro-beads instead of heat or glue. A versatile option that can be moved up as your hair grows.

Bellami Tape-Ins

Thin, flat panels that lie close to the scalp. A quick, lightweight way to add volume and length.

Bellami Wefts

Rows of hair that add fullness and length, from subtle volume to dramatic transformations.

Removals

Gentle removal of extensions, including ones installed at another salon, so you can start fresh or switch methods.

How It Works

  1. 1. Consultation

    We assess your hair's texture, density and health, talk through your goals, recommend a method and color-match your extensions. You'll leave with a custom quote. The $50 consultation fee goes toward your service.

  2. 2. Installation

    Your custom hair is installed, then cut and blended into your natural hair and styled. You'll leave with aftercare instructions for your method.

  3. 3. Maintenance

    Regular move-ups keep your extensions comfortable and hidden as your hair grows. For NBR, that's every 6–8 weeks.
