Kerasilk Keratin: For Smoothing and Frizz Control

Houston's notorious humidity makes frizz control a top priority for many of our clients. The Kerasilk Keratin collection delivers long-lasting smoothness and manageability without sacrificing movement or natural texture.

Shampoo & Conditioner:

Kerasilk Keratin Smooth Medium Shampoo & Conditioner

This transformative duo gently cleanses while infusing hair with keratin for progressive smoothing benefits. For my Houston clients battling humidity-induced frizz, these products provide cumulative improvement in hair manageability and shine.

Treatment Product:

Kerasilk Keratin Shape Medium

This at-home intensive treatment extends the results of salon keratin services while providing exceptional smoothing benefits on its own. The silk-infused formula dramatically reduces blow-dry time—a significant advantage in our busy lives.

Styling Essential:

Kerasilk Keratin Smooth Control Humidity Barrier Spray

This finishing spray creates an invisible shield against Houston's humidity while providing lightweight, flexible hold. It's particularly effective during our most challenging summer months for maintaining sleek, smooth styles.

Stylist Tip:

For maximum frizz control in Houston's climate, combine the Keratin collection with our in-salon Kerasilk Keratin Treatment. This professional service delivers up to 5 months of dramatically smoother, more manageable hair—perfect for those seeking a more permanent solution to humidity challenges.

Kerasilk Reconstruct: For Damaged and Processed Hair

For clients with damaged, over-processed hair from coloring, heat styling, or chemical treatments, the Kerasilk Reconstruct collection offers intensive repair from within.

Shampoo & Conditioner:

Kerasilk Reconstruct Shampoo & Conditioner

This restorative duo doesn't just mask damage—it works to reconstruct the hair fiber from within. The innovative keratin and hyaloveil complex penetrates deeply to rebuild damaged areas while providing immediate surface improvement.

Weekly Treatment:

Kerasilk Reconstruct Intensive Repair Mask

This deeply restorative treatment dramatically improves hair's resilience and elasticity with just one use. For severely damaged hair, I recommend using this weekly mask for at least one month to see transformative results.

Leave-In Protection:

This protective spray not only speeds up drying time but also shields hair from heat damage up to 446°F (230°C). It's particularly valuable for those who heat style regularly, creating an invisible barrier against further damage.

Kerasilk Repower: For Thinning Hair and Volume

Hair thinning and loss of density affect many clients, regardless of age or gender. The Kerasilk Repower collection addresses these concerns with innovative technology that creates immediate volume while supporting healthier hair growth over time.

Shampoo & Conditioner:

Kerasilk Repower Volume Shampoo & Conditioner

This volumizing duo creates impressive body and fullness without weighing down even the finest hair. The microlight texture technology expands hair from within while strengthening each strand from root to tip.

Scalp Treatment:

Kerasilk Repower Intensive Anti-Hairloss Spray

This leave-in treatment targets the root causes of hair thinning with a combination of caffeine, zinc, and niacinamide. When used consistently, it helps reduce hair loss while encouraging stronger, thicker growth.

Volume Booster:

Kerasilk Repower Volume Blow-Dry Spray

This weightless spray creates exceptional volume and lift at the roots without stiffness or stickiness. For my fine-haired clients, it's become an essential styling step for creating lasting body that resists Houston's humidity.

Benefits for Thinning Hair:

  • Addresses both immediate appearance and long-term health
  • Strengthens hair from within to reduce breakage
  • Supports scalp health to encourage new growth
  • Creates natural-looking volume without product buildup

Building Your Kerasilk Routine

With multiple collections addressing different hair concerns, creating your ideal Kerasilk routine can feel overwhelming. Here are my recommendations for building a perfectly tailored regimen:

Essential Three-Product Routine:

If you're new to Kerasilk or looking to streamline your routine, start with these three fundamentals:

  1. Shampoo & Conditioner from the collection that addresses your primary concern
  2. One leave-in or protective product specific to your hair needs
  3. One styling product that supports your desired finished look

This simplified approach delivers impressive results while introducing you to the Kerasilk experience.

Comprehensive Kerasilk Routine:

For those ready to fully embrace the Kerasilk transformation, here's a complete routine structure:

  1. Cleanse: Shampoo & conditioner from your primary Kerasilk collection
  2. Treat: Weekly intensive mask or treatment for deeper transformation
  3. Prep: Leave-in treatment, heat protectant, or primer before styling
  4. Style: Specific styling product for your desired finished look
  5. Finish: Setting spray, humidity barrier, or protective oil as final touch

Combination Approach:

Many clients find that combining products from different Kerasilk collections offers the best results for multiple concerns. Popular combinations include:

  • Color + Reconstruct: For color-treated hair with damage concerns
  • Keratin + Repower: For fine hair that needs both smoothing and volume
  • Control + Color: For curly, color-treated hair in Houston's climate
